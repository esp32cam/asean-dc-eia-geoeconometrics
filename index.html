What the run showed

QuestionResult from the run
Does non-random disclosure bias portfolio totals? Yes. Disclosers' true average PUE was 1.34 against 1.54 for non-disclosers. Applying the disclosed average understated portfolio CO2 by 5.1 percent.
Can the bias be corrected without disclosure? Partly by weighting, better by selection correction. Inverse-probability weighting left 4.0 percent bias, the Heckman two-step 2.7 percent, and the engineering prior with Monte Carlo 1.2 percent.
Are the uncertainty bands honest? Broadly. On 47 facilities that began disclosing after the estimate, nominal 90 percent intervals achieved 77 to 83 percent coverage depending on the method.
Is the footprint spatially clustered? Yes. Facility-level Moran's I on estimated CO2 was +0.28 with a permutation p-value of 0.001, so national thresholds leak across borders.
Does the policy design recover known effects? Yes. Difference-in-differences recovered the planted Singapore moratorium, Johor spillover and Johor vetting effects almost exactly.
How much load do today's rules actually capture? A full assessment covers 20 percent of projects but only 26 percent of estimated CO2 and 25 percent of water.
Could a resource-based rule do better? Yes. A threshold of "IT load at least 20 MW or water use at least 300 m³/day" captured 84 percent of CO2 and 93 percent of water.

Read this before quoting any number

The facility data are illustrative, not a real register. Hub coordinates and hub context are anchored to the real world, but every facility and its attributes are generated so that estimators can be scored against a known truth. Country grid factors, tariffs, carbon-price paths and the non-Thai screening thresholds are labelled placeholders.

Legal status is mid-August 2026, including the Thai Prime Minister's Office Regulation on the Data Center Business Policy Committee B.E. 2569 published in the Royal Gazette on 13 August 2026. Rules that could not be confirmed are flagged UNVERIFIED in the screening engine rather than guessed. These are the applicability of the planning-office building threshold to a pure data-centre building, whether standby generators count toward the Factory Act horsepower test, and the volumetric thresholds for water and groundwater licences.

One statistical caveat. The Heckman selection term is correctly signed but not statistically significant at this sample size, which is what disclosure samples of roughly a hundred facilities typically deliver in the region.
